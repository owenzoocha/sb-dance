About us

More than a school. A family.Since 2001.

A friendly, inclusive dance school in Pencoed, Bridgend, with a new branch in Aberthin, Cowbridge. We welcome boys and girls from age 3, and adults too.

Every dancer supported, every dancer stretched.

We're a friendly and supportive school where every child and adult is part of the family. Classes follow the I.S.T.D. (Imperial Society of Teachers of Dancing) syllabus, so dancers build real technique and can take graded exams.

Exams are encouraged but never compulsory. There's an annual show for everyone, and our competition team, by invitation only, has represented Team Wales at the Dance World Cup and won awards at festivals and local competitions.
