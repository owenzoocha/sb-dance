CDMT Recognised Teacher

Safe, qualified, trusted teaching.

Sarah is recognised by the Council for Dance, Drama and Musical Theatre (CDMT), which quality-assures performing arts training. Recognised Teachers put pupils' safety first and meet six requirements of good professional practice.

01

Safety

Health and safety, safe dance practice, safeguarding, equal opportunities and customer service policies in place.

02

Qualified

A government-recognised teaching qualification at Level 3 or above, or a performance degree with QTS or QTLS.

03

Conduct

Follows the CDMT Code of Professional Conduct and Practice.

04

DBS

Holds a valid Disclosure and Barring Service certificate.

05

Clarity

Makes the Six Requirements publicly available.

06

Insured

Holds appropriate and adequate insurance.
