RulesBalletTapModern, Jazz & AcroOther classesWhere to buy

Hair & jewellery, every class

Hair must be neatly tied back in plaits or a bun at all times. Stud earrings only, and no other jewellery.

Uniform

What to wear

01

Ballet

Girls · Primary to Grade 2

  • Bluebell short-sleeve skirted leotard
  • Pink socks
  • Ballet shoes

Girls · Grade 3 and up

  • Blue leotard
  • Pink ballet tights
  • Pink ballet shoes

Grade 5 and up: a black camisole leotard is optional.

Boys

  • White ballet leotard
  • Navy tights
  • White ballet shoes
02

Tap

Girls

  • Rosie, Ellie or Evie leotard
  • Black jazz pants, or black dance tights with dance shorts or leggings
  • Black socks
  • Black tap shoes

Boys

  • Black sports t-shirt
  • Black sports shorts or black dance joggers
  • Black socks
  • Black tap shoes
03

Modern Theatre, Jazz & Acro

Girls

  • Rosie, Ellie or Evie leotard
  • Crop top: Zoe, Megan or long-sleeve
  • Black dance tights
  • Dance shorts or leggings

Boys

  • Black sports t-shirt
  • Black sports shorts or dance joggers
04

Other classes

Students may wear their own leotards in school colours.
